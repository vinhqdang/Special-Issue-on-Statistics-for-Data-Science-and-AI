\section{Ablations: full tables}
\label{sec:app-ablations}

This appendix gives the per-cell results behind Section~\ref{sec:ablations}: 29 setting--budget cells, $R=150$ replications and $B=999$ bootstrap resamples. All values are stored in
\path{results/label_ablations.json}, which is produced by the script
\path{experiments/label_ablations.py}; its \texttt{report} mode prints the tables and draws the figures. Abbreviations: Credit = Credit card default, Law = Law school, Elec.\ = Electricity.

\paragraph{Implementation details.} Stratifiers: the binary stratifier crosses group with the indicator $0<K_i<M$. The three-model pattern uses the models with the highest agreement between $\hat y_{mi}$ and $\mathbf 1\{\pi_i\ge 0.5\}$ (chosen without labels; e.g.\ histogram GB, random forest and MLP in Adult-sex), with the eight patterns ordered by their number of ones so that merging of small strata goes to a neighbouring pattern. Proxy bins split each $(G,K)$ cell at the quantiles of $\pi_i$ within the cell. All stratifiers merge strata below 50 units within group, as in the default. When $H$ strata at 10 labels would use more than 80\% of the budget, the floor is lowered to $\max(2,\lfloor 0.8L/H\rfloor)$. This affects only the proxy-bin stratifiers at $L\le 500$ (floors 3--9) and the grid designs with floor 20 at $L=250$ (floors 10--18). Proxies: ``single model'' uses the predicted probabilities of the first logistic regression or of Gaussian naive Bayes; ``noise'' adds $N(0,2^2)$ to $\mathrm{logit}(\pi_i)$; ``shuffle'' permutes $\pi$ within each group (fixed seed). Satterthwaite degrees of freedom are $\nu=(\sum_h a_h s^2_{z,h})^2/\sum_h (a_h s^2_{z,h})^2/(n_h-1)$ with $a_h=N_h^2(1-n_h/N_h)/n_h$, computed per target. The bootstrap resamples $n_h$ units with replacement within each stratum and takes the 2.5\% and 97.5\% quantiles of the plug-in estimate. The proxy variance floor is defined in Section~\ref{sec:ablations}. A3 subsets were drawn once per size with seed 20261104 and are the same in every setting.
