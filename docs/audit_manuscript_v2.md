Scope: main.tex, sections/*.tex, tables/*.tex, references.bib. Every quantitative claim in the abstract, introduction, empirical, baselines, ablations, simulation, discussion, guidance and appendix text was recomputed from results/*.json with scripts kept in the scratchpad (a1.py, a2.py, a3.py, b1.py, c1-c5.py, d1-d2.py, e1-e3.py, cr.py, ax.py, pm.py). ## 3. Unresolved or for the authors (file:line, current line numbers)

- design.tex:23 and discussion.tex:5. "predictions here are calibrated and informative" / "average predicted probability of nine models is calibrated": no calibration check of the ensemble mean is reported anywhere (only the ablation shows that single-model and naive-Bayes proxies do worse). - introduction.tex:15. StratPPI "higher efficiency than PATTERN in 24 of the 35 cells" is from the main study (per-cell medians against SRS). The same comparison in the baselines study (other seeds, paired ratios) is 19 of 35 (baselines.tex:44) and the paired ratio in the main study is 18 of 35. The metric and the study should be named in the introduction.
- simulation.tex:31. "median efficiencies coincide with results/label_efficiency.json ... so the per-target efficiencies below are those underlying the main results": true for ten pools; for law school the main study (label_efficiency_dif.json: 16.4, 37.6, 60.4, 1e26) uses a different set of compared estimands from the old file (10.2, 15.3, 20.7, 4e23). Needs a sentence, or rerun of theory_check.py with the live-target mask.
- setting.tex:31. "an earlier analysis of the same data settings" is based on twelve settings (Nomao included, as the introduction says); the present paper has eleven.
- setting.tex:4. Pinpoint "Chapter 13" of Sarndal et al. (1992) for the superpopulation extension was not verified.

- Bibliography: duplicate entries for the same works (bennett2010 / bennett2010online, kumar2018 / kumar2018classifier, musgrove2023 / musgrove2023discordant; sawade2012 duplicates sawade2012active and is unused, wu2001 is unused).
